What the words mean

The jargon, in plain English

You'll see these terms on every laptop listing. Here's what each one does for you.

GPU

Graphics card

Draws everything you see on screen. A stronger one keeps 3D models smooth and makes renders much faster. Its own memory (6GB on A by HP's spec, 8GB on B) helps with big, detailed scenes.

CPU

Processor

The laptop's brain. It does most of the work when you model in SolidWorks. Both have fast Intel Core i9 processors. B's is two generations newer, with more cores, so it's quicker at heavy modelling jobs.

RAM

Memory

Lets you keep lots of things open at once, like SolidWorks, Photoshop and a browser. 32GB on A, which is plenty. B has 16GB, which is fine to start, and it uses memory slots rather than soldered chips, so it can be upgraded later.

SSD

Storage

Where your files and software live. Design files and renders add up, so more is better. 1TB on A is roomy; 2TB on B is more than you'll need.

Rendering

Making a render

Turning your 3D model into a realistic, photo-like image with materials and lighting. Used for presentation boards, crits and your portfolio. KeyShot and SolidWorks Visualize do this.

Certified

Pro graphics card

Both have NVIDIA pro graphics cards that SolidWorks has tested and approved. That means fewer glitches than a gaming laptop, and the realistic preview mode, RealView, works properly.

When you buy

Checklist

  1. 01
    Check the spec yourself before you buy

    Listings can change, and titles don't always match the details. On the listing, check:

    • Memory (RAM): 32GB on A, 16GB on B
    • Storage (SSD): 1TB on A, 2TB on B
    • Graphics: RTX A3000 on A, RTX 2000 Ada on B. Not a T1200 or a GeForce card.
    • Grade: see the grades section above
    • Windows: Windows 11 Pro
    • Keyboard: UK layout. B's listing says so; CeX doesn't say for A. A UK keyboard has a £ sign on the 3 key and a tall, L-shaped Enter key. If A arrives with a different layout, you can return it within 14 days.

    When we checked on 1 November 2026: A was £822.95 including delivery and B was £1,299 with free delivery.

  2. 02
    Check the basket total

    Make sure it matches the price above before paying.

  3. 03
    Don't buy SolidWorks

    It should come free from the university. Check with the course before paying for a licence or a laptop bundle that includes one.
