The short version

Both run SolidWorks well. The difference is rendering versus weight.

Our pick: ThinkPad P15 Gen 2

Industrial design is marked heavily on how your work looks, which means lots of rendering: turning a 3D model into a realistic, photo-like image for boards, crits and your portfolio. The P15 Gen 2 does that much faster.

The P15v Gen 3 is the better choice if you'll carry it every day and do your heavy rendering on university computers. It's lighter, thinner and £175 cheaper.

What the words mean

The jargon, in plain English

You'll see these terms on every laptop listing. Here's what each one does for you.

GPU

Graphics card

Draws everything you see on screen. A stronger one keeps 3D models smooth and makes renders much faster. This is the biggest difference between the two laptops.

CPU

Processor

The laptop's brain. It does most of the work when you model in SolidWorks. Both have fast Intel Core i7 processors; the Gen 3's is newer and a little quicker.

RAM

Memory

Lets you keep lots of things open at once, like SolidWorks, Photoshop and a browser. 32GB on both, which is plenty.

SSD

Storage

Where your files and software live. Design files and renders add up, so more is better. 512GB is fine; 1TB is roomy.

Rendering

Making a render

Turning your 3D model into a realistic, photo-like image with materials and lighting. Used for presentation boards, crits and your portfolio. Software like KeyShot does this.

Certified

Pro graphics card

Both laptops have NVIDIA pro graphics cards that SolidWorks has tested and approved. That means fewer glitches than a gaming laptop, and the realistic preview mode, RealView, works properly.

Carrying it around

Weight in your bag

Both are proper workstations, so neither is light. The difference is about a litre bottle of water, every day.

When you buy

Checklist

  1. 01
    Check the spec yourself before you buy

    Listings can change, and the title doesn't always match the options picked. On the product page, check each drop-down is set to what you want:

    • Memory (RAM): 32GB
    • Storage (SSD): ideally 1TB; 512GB will be OK
    • Graphics: RTX A3000 on the Gen 2, Quadro T1200 on the Gen 3
    • Grade: see the grades guide below
    • Windows: Windows 11 Pro

    When we checked on 31 October, the Gen 2 was £799.99 with 32GB and 1TB. The Gen 3 was £624.99 with 32GB and 512GB, or £684.99 with 1TB. If something on the page is unclear, ask pcrenewed before ordering.

  2. 02
    Check the basket total

    Make sure it matches the price above before paying.

  3. 03
    Don't buy SolidWorks

    It should come free from the university. Check with the course before paying for a licence or a laptop bundle that includes one.
